# Community conduct

This learning project welcomes questions and corrections from people at different
levels of experience. Address the argument, derivation or code with patience and
respect. Explain mistakes in a way that helps the author and future readers learn.

We expect contributors to acknowledge uncertainty, credit others' work, respect
privacy and accept good-faith corrections. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
personal attacks, spam and publishing someone else's private information are not
acceptable in project discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or
close a discussion to keep the project constructive. Decisions should be
proportionate and based on conduct, not on disagreement about physics or design.

Report a public conduct issue through GitHub's reporting controls or ask the
maintainer [KrAzad0](https://github.com/KrAzad0) for a private reporting channel.
Do not post sensitive details publicly. No dedicated confidential reporting
address or response-time guarantee is currently established.
